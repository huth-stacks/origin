A familiar wallet connection.

Untrusted claim

The wallet refuses to repeat an attacker’s story.

The middle link carries an identity claim, but its source is not trusted by Leather. The claimed organization name and role are quarantined instead of rendered as truth.

Wallet language “Unrecognized identity claim” is precise: a claim exists, but Leather does not endorse it.
Signal

Trusted attestation

The same transaction surface now has attributable identity.

For the exact Acme contract principal, Leather finds an active claim from a trusted Testnet attester. The wallet can safely show the organization and role.

Still not a safety badge The interface states that identity does not guarantee the contract or transaction is safe.
Identity

06

Separate heuristic

Unknown does not mean harmless.

The right-hand contract has no trusted attestation. Its similarity to the trusted Acme contract triggers a distinct lookalike warning without changing the provenance verdict.

Two independent facts Origin is unknown. A lookalike heuristic also found something worth the user’s attention.

The full story

One request. Three honest outcomes.

Leather derives the contract principal itself, queries the Origin registry, applies its own trust policy, and presents provenance without pretending to certify safety.
